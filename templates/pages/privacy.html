Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Rentorra Inc.

1. Overview

At Rentorra ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible from rentorra.com, protecting the privacy of our visitors and home-seeking clients is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is utilized, and your choices regarding your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when submitting a property requirement, scheduling a site visit, contacting us via phone or WhatsApp, or registering an enquiry. This includes:

  • Full Name and Contact Mobile / Phone Number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Preferred rental locations, BHK preferences, and monthly budget parameters
  • Technical logs: IP address, device type, browser information, and referral UTM campaign source tags

3. How We Use Your Data

The primary purpose of data collection is lead fulfillment and tenant advisory:

  • To coordinate physical site visits with verified property owners and managers.
  • To contact you through phone, SMS, or WhatsApp regarding matching rental listings.
  • To prevent fraudulent submissions, spam, or duplicate automated inquiries.
  • To measure marketing channel effectiveness (Google, Facebook, Instagram ads).

4. Zero Third-Party Selling Guarantee

Rentorra does not sell, rent, or trade user data to unaffiliated third-party marketing companies, telemarketers, or financial brokers.
